📖 New: Azeroth-Tagebuch – an adventure journal for World of Warcraft

A small addon records what your characters experience, and the app (Mac & Windows) turns it into a journal and dashboard – live while you play:
• quests and your routes on the map
• dungeons & delves: runs, bosses, tiers, keys and loot
• DPS/HPS from the official combat log
• loot with drop rates, bank & warband bank contents

Free and open source, English & German, no ads, no tracking. It only reads files – no automation, nothing that touches the game.

⬇️ github.com/dirkkredinger/azeroth-tagebuch
